Definition of DoneKB is a governed evidence system, not just a folder of PDFs.All published sources carry authority, scope, provenance and lifecycle state.Vector-only retrieval is not the production retrieval strategy.Fault knowledge exists as structured objects in addition to source documents.Historical cases retain confirmed cause, action and outcome.Pump/equipment references support exact lookup and source traceability.Knowledge Service exposes stable and testable contracts.Agent accesses KB only through typed tools/services.Tenant ACL and document-as-data/prompt-injection controls are implemented.KB/index versions are reproducible and rollbackable.Retrieval/fault/citation/authority benchmarks are recorded before publication.21. Final Phase 3 BoundaryPhase 3 ends when the Knowledge Foundation is reliable enough that a later Agent workflow can request knowledge by objective, receive ranked and authority-filtered evidence, cite the exact source, compare structured fault/case objects, and operate without directly querying KB storage. Runtime asset context, live telemetry, deterministic calculations and ML outputs remain separate evidence sources and are intentionally not merged into the KB itself.
