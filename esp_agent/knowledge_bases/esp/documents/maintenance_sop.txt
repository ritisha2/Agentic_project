STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: ESP MAINTENANCE & REPAIR

SOP 101: Motor Bearing Replacement Procedure
- Scope: Applies to downhole motor bearing overhaul and replacement.
- Required Tools: Bearing puller, hydraulic press, torque wrench, micrometer set.
- Required Parts: Bearing assembly P/N ABC-456.
- Safety Precautions: Isolate electrical supply at surface drive unit, lockout-tagout (LOTO), verify wellhead pressure is zero psi.
- Step-by-step Execution:
  1. Dismantle motor housing end bell.
  2. Remove old bearings using approved hydraulic bearing puller.
  3. Clean shaft and seat area thoroughly with solvent.
  4. Install new bearings (P/N ABC-456) using hydraulic press.
  5. Verify shaft runout and alignment with dial indicator.
  6. Reassemble motor end bell and torque bolts to 85 ft-lbs.
- Expected Duration: 4 hours.
