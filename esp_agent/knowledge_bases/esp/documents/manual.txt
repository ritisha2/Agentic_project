ELECTRIC SUBMERSIBLE PUMP (ESP) SYSTEM OPERATING MANUAL

1. System Overview
Electric Submersible Pumps (ESPs) are multi-stage centrifugal pumps powered by a downhole electric motor. ESPs operate in fluid-producing wells to lift oil, water, and gas mixtures to the surface.

2. Standard Operating Ranges & Alarm Thresholds
- Motor Temperature:
  * Normal Operating Range: 80.0°C to 120.0°C
  * Warning Threshold: > 130.0°C (High motor temperature alert)
  * Critical Threshold: > 150.0°C (Emergency shutdown required)

- Intake Pressure (PIP):
  * Normal Operating Range: 200.0 psi to 500.0 psi
  * Warning Threshold: < 150.0 psi (Gas lock risk / Pump-off condition warning)
  * Critical Threshold: < 100.0 psi (Severe pump cavitation / Low pressure trip)

- Radial Vibration:
  * Normal Operating Range: 0.5 g to 1.5 g
  * Warning Threshold: > 3.0 g (Bearing wear / Imbalance alert)
  * Critical Threshold: > 5.0 g (Structural damage risk / Mechanical trip)

- Axial Vibration:
  * Normal Operating Range: 0.1 g to 1.0 g
  * Warning Threshold: > 1.5 g

3. Installation & Pre-Commissioning Procedures
Ensure electrical isolation and mechanical check of all motor leads. Verify static reservoir pressure and ensure downhole sensor telemetry is calibrated prior to starting the drive controller.

4. Normal Operating Procedures
Operate drive within recommended frequency limits (45 Hz to 65 Hz). Maintain intake pressure above minimum liquid level to prevent gas lock. Monitor motor winding temperature continuously.
