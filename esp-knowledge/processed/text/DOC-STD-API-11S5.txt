Caledonian Cables has continually expanded its global presence in Europe and Asia.
Caledonian & Addison, produces a wide range of cables 
for communication, power and electronics in its primary plants in UK, Italy and Spain. Our extensive global network of manufacturing facilities gives us significant scale and the flexibility

Caledonian & Addison has been respected for its high standards of quality, excellent service

Our Design Centers work closely with customers to constantly improve its standard range of

Caledonian & Addison has established an extensive network of design, manufacturing, and logistics facilities in the world’s major markets to serve the growing outsourcing needs of both multinational and regional customers.
