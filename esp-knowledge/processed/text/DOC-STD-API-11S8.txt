1 Scope
This Recommended Practice (RP) provides guidelines to est ablish consistency in the cont rol and analysis of electric 
submersible pump (ESP) system vibrati ons. This document is considered appropr iate for the testin g of ESP systems 
and subsystems for the majori ty of ESP applications.
This RP covers the vibration limits, testi ng, and analysis of ESP systems and subsystems.
