ADVAIT ESP -PMM | Intern Onboarding Confidential Working Draft02 / ARTIFICIAL LIFT BASICS
Why ESP exists in oil production
When reservoir energy is not enough, artificial lift supplies the missing pressure to move fluids to surface.
ESP = electric motor + multistage centrifugal 
pump downhole
High -volume artificial lift for wells that cannot flow economically 
by natural reservoir pressure.
Typically selected for moderate -to-high liquid rate wells where rod -
pump or gas -lift economics are weak.
The system converts electrical energy into hydraulic head inside 
the well.
Software analogy: ESP is a cyber -physical production service with hard constraints
Source: Electrical Submersible Pumps Manual snippets; ESP Run Life Factors slide deck.

ADVAIT ESP -PMM | Intern Onboarding Confidential Working Draft04 / WORKING PRINCIPLE
How the ESP actually pumps
Each stage adds a small pressure increase; many stages stacked together create the total developed head.
Stage -level physics
1Fluid enters the impeller from pump intake.
2Impeller accelerates fluid outward, adding velocity.
3Diffuser converts velocity into pressure.
4Stages repeat until discharge pressure is achieved.
Source: ESP Components Equipment Description, pages 6 –7; SLB Defining Series.

ADVAIT ESP -PMM | Intern Onboarding Confidential Working Draft05 / PERFORMANCE CURVES
Pump curve is the operating contract
The AI model must know whether the current point is inside the recommended operating range, not just whether values are high 
or low.
What the curve tells us
Flow rate vs head: hydraulic capability
Horsepower: motor load requirement
Efficiency: best operating zone
Operating point: actual production reality
Left of range: downthrust/low -flow risk; right of range: 
upthrust/high -flow risk
Core AI feature: deviation from design / BEP / ROR
Source: SLB Defining Series page 1; pressure -gradient design slides.

=== PAGE 7 ===
ADVAIT ESP -PMM | Intern Onboarding Confidential Working Draft06 / WELL HYDRAULICSTotal Dynamic Head links well conditions to pump 
demandTDH is where production engineering meets sensor data.
TDH = vertical lift + friction + 
wellhead pressure
Net vertical lift changes with producing fluid level.
Tubing friction rises with flow rate and fluid 
properties.
Wellhead pressure/backpressure shifts the required 
discharge pressure.
Wrong density, water cut or GOR assumptions break 
the model.
Implementation note: create a TDH calculation service, keep every 
assumption visible, and compare calculated PDP/PIP with gauge 
values.
Source: ESP design Total Dynamic Head material; pressure -gradient curves.

Run life is limited by the weakest operating factor
Failure is rarely “one variable high”; it is usually a context -dependent combination.
Key degradation drivers
Wrong sizing or operating outside ROR
High bottom -hole / motor temperature
Free gas and gas lock / gas interference
Sand / solids abrasion and erosion
Corrosion and scale deposition
Electrical failures and power quality problems
Operational cycling, frequent starts/stops

=== PAGE 12 ===
ADVAIT ESP -PMM | Intern Onboarding Confidential Working Draft11 / SIGNATURES
Troubleshooting is pattern recognition with physics
The same symptom can mean different faults; use multiple parameters together.
Example pattern families
Reduced/no flow + rising PIP + high temperature: 
blockage, broken shaft or shutdown -at-surface 
scenarios.
Erratic amps and pressure with reduced flow: free gas at 
pump intake.
Reduced flow + elevated amps + reduced discharge 
pressure: possible stage wear.
Increasing frequency should raise flow, WHP , amps, PDP 
and temperature unless constrained.
Source: ChampionX/Unbridled ESP Troubleshooting Guide page 1.

Current signature features: imbalance, THD proxy,
